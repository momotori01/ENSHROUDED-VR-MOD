# Enshrouded VR - Motion Controls

## Features
Room-scale motion controls.

- Melee attacks are made by swinging your sword.
- Every cooldown is taken from the vanilla animations, so the mod does not make you overpowered.
- In the air, the rising slash and the plunging attack are told apart by how you swing the weapon.
- Skill attacks move you as far as they do in vanilla. You still have to land the hit with your own sword.
- You draw the bow yourself.
- Throwing: hold your left hand out in front of you and press the right trigger to show the throw guide. Then just throw.
- Heavy attacks: swing while holding the right trigger.
- Shields guard while you hold the left grip. The guard faces the way the shield's face points.
- Parry: at the moment you raise the guard, or by flicking your left hand quickly while you hold the guard.
- Two-handed weapons: hold the handle with the left grip and attack by swinging the right hand. Hold the weapon out in front with both hands to guard.
- Tools such as the pickaxe and the axe are swung too. You aim with the direction of your right hand.
- Fishing rods cast at the moment you release the trigger during the swing. A stronger swing casts farther.
- Weapons are drawn and put away at your body: left shoulder + left grip = bow, right shoulder + right grip = your current weapon, left hip + left grip = shield.
- Crouching in real life makes you sneak (switch it on/off and set the depth in the in-game "VR settings").
- The crosshair and gauges appear where your right hand points. The HUD fades out after 5 seconds without changes and comes back when your health and the like change.
- Your body moves with IK. Other players who have the mod see your VR movements as they are (even if they are not playing in VR themselves). You can still play together with people who do not have the mod.
- The title screen and the menus are shown on a panel in front of you and operated with the controller's laser pointer.

## Controls
### Movement
- Left stick: move (forward is where you look)
- Right stick left/right: turn
- Right stick up: sprint / sneak
- Right stick down: dodge
- Right A: jump
- Right B: short press to interact (pick up / examine), hold for the quick slots (choose with your hand's direction and release)

### Combat
- Swing a weapon or tool: attack (swing while holding the right trigger for a heavy attack)
- Left grip: guard / hold a two-handed weapon
- Right trigger: draw the bow, throw, cast the fishing rod

### Menus and more
- While a hand is held against your temple: the right stick becomes the D-pad, pressing the right stick opens the menu, pressing the left stick goes back
- While holding the building hammer and pressing left B: choose what to build (choose with the right stick, release to confirm)
- Reset the view: look at both palms in front of your face for 3 seconds (the headset's own reset works too)
- "!VR bug report button!" in the start menu: saves the 30 seconds around the press and pictures of both eyes into a zip. Please use it to report problems.

## Installation
1. Start the Steam version of Enshrouded normally once, then quit.
2. In Steam: Manage -> Browse local files.
3. Put everything in this package into the folder that contains enshrouded.exe.
4. With the game closed, open "Enshrouded VR setting.exe" and choose "VR" (VR is off right after installing).
5. Start SteamVR first, then start the game from Steam.

For details, see EnshroudedVR\README.txt in the package.

## Requirements
- The game version the package is made for (EnshroudedVR\version.txt). When the game is updated, the mod switches itself off and the game runs without it until a new package is out.
- An NVIDIA graphics card that supports DLSS
- An OpenXR runtime (tested with SteamVR)
